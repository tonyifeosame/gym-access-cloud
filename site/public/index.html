Access management software and fingerprint terminals, working as one system.

AccessLink is an access control platform. Fingerprint terminals at doors, gates and turnstiles decide who may enter, and the AccessLink Console is where your team manages the people, sites and rules behind those decisions.

Learn more Read the API documentation

AccessLink is in pre-launch. No hardware or availability details have been published.

How it works

Two parts: the software you manage access in, and the terminals that enforce it.

  1. Manage access in the console

    Add the people who may enter, organise your locations into sites, and set the schedules that say who may go where and when. Owners, administrators, managers and viewers each see what their role allows.

  2. Decide at the door

    Fingerprint terminals at doors, gates and turnstiles check each person against the rules for their site, and every admission or refusal is recorded in an event log you can review in the console.

  3. Connect your own systems

    A documented API lets a membership platform, booking system or HR tool manage the people who may enter and read what happened at the door, without someone at the console. Read the API documentation.
